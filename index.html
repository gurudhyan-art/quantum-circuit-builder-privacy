Privacy Policy — Quantum Circuit Builder

Last updated: October 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Quantum Circuit Builder is an Android application for creating, editing, saving, opening, and simulating quantum circuits. This policy explains how the application handles information.

2. Information Collection

The app does not collect personal information, require user accounts, or use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services. The app does not send circuit data to a developer-operated server or cloud service.

3. Circuit Files and Local Storage

Users can save and open .qcb circuit files. Files are stored locally on the device or in a location selected by the user through Android's file-selection features. The developer does not receive these files. Users can delete saved files using their device's file-management tools.

4. Sharing of Information

The app does not sell, rent, or share users' personal information. Android, Google Play, or a file-storage provider selected by the user may process information under their own privacy policies when their services are used.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Circuit files remain wherever users choose to save them until deleted by the user. The app does not maintain a developer-operated account or cloud database containing user circuits.

6. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

Circuit files are stored in locations selected by the user. Users should protect their devices and exported files appropriately.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this URL.
